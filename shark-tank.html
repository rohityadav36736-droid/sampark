Why The Problem Had To Be Solved

In Indian cities like Delhi, Gurgaon, and Noida, double-parking and tight parking spaces are unavoidable. But the traditional solutions caused even worse problems.

📄

1. The Paper Slip Privacy Leak

Leaving handwritten slips on dashboards exposes personal numbers to passersby, telemarketers, and fraudsters. Women drivers in particular face severe harassment.

🤬

2. Road Rage & Towing Gridlocks

When cars are blocked, tempers flare. Traffic police tow vehicles when owners cannot be contacted immediately, leading to massive fines and vehicle damage.

🛡️

3. The Sampark Telephony Shield

A caller scans the windshield QR tag with any standard smartphone camera. The call routes through our cloud telephony proxy. Both numbers remain 100% hidden.
