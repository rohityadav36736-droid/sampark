Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sampark masked calling, privacy architecture, and delivery.

1. Does the person scanning my vehicle tag need to download any mobile app?
+

No app is needed! Anyone with an iPhone camera, Android camera, Google Lens, or Paytm/Google Pay QR scanner can instantly scan the tag. It opens a lightweight secure browser web app that connects the masked call or WhatsApp alert within 3 seconds.

2. How does masked calling keep my personal mobile number 100% private?
+

When a scanner taps "Call Vehicle Owner", our bank-grade cloud telephony exchange initiates a dual-leg virtual call. Both parties see an encrypted virtual relay number (+91 11-4084-XXXX). Your actual personal mobile number is never displayed, stored, or revealed to the caller.

3. Can my family members receive emergency SOS accident alerts?
+

Yes! Through your Sampark Driver Portal, you can link up to 3 emergency contacts (spouse, parents, siblings). If a bystander or police officer scans your tag and presses "Emergency SOS", an immediate SMS and WhatsApp broadcast with the vehicle's exact GPS location is dispatched to your family.

4. How fast is delivery across Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, and NCR?
+

All orders placed before 3:00 PM IST are dispatched on the same day from our Saket Delhi Logistics Hub via Delhivery Express. Drivers in Delhi NCR typically receive their package within 24 to 48 hours with full Cash on Delivery support.

5. Is there any monthly subscription fee or hidden charges?
+

None whatsoever. You only pay a one-time fee of ₹199 (discounted from ₹499) for the physical acrylic tag, lifetime QR hosting, and unlimited masked calls for your registered vehicle.

6. Will the tag peel off in 48°C Delhi summer heat or during car washes?
+

No! Sampark tags are crafted from industrial-grade dual-layer acrylic resin with high-strength automotive 3M optical adhesive. They adhere to the inside of your front or rear windshield, safe from rain, windshield wipers, dust storms, and extreme Delhi heat.
